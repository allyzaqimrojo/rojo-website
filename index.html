About Wafflelicious

Who We Are

Wafflelicious is a local dessert business based in Valencia City, Bukidnon dedicated to offering delicious, high-quality, and affordable Waffloat treats. We aim to bring our products closer to more customers by providing creative dessert options that combine familiar flavors with a unique twist.

Our Story

Established in 2025, Wafflelicious began with the idea of giving the classic float a new twist by replacing traditional graham crackers with fluffy waffles. This inspired the creation of Waffloat, a dessert that combines soft waffle layers with rich and creamy flavors.
