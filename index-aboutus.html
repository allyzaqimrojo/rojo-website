About Wafflelicious

Wafflelicious Company is a local dessert partnership based in Valencia City, Bukidnon. We offer Waffloat, a delightful dessert that combines fluffy waffle layers with creamy and flavorful ingredients. With our tagline, "Taste the satisfaction", we aim to bring customers an enjoyable and affordable dessert experience, with each serving priced at ₱20.

Our Concept

Waffloat puts a creative twist on the familiar dessert float by replacing traditional graham layers with fluffy waffles. We combine different flavors and ingredients to create a sweet, satisfying treat.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, enjoyable, and high-quality dessert creations while offering customers a delightful experience through creative flavors and satisfying combinations.

Our Vision

To establish Wafflelicious as a recognized local dessert brand, reach more customers through online platforms, and create opportunities for business growth and future partnerships.
